Description

Details on material used for dating. Material may be either connected to a specific taxon (or pseudotaxon if at higher taxonomic level than species) or described in the material_dated text field. Specific abundance elements (e.g. seed, elytron) may also be selected. A date may consist of multiple dated materials.
